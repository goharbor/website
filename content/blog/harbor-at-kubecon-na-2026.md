# Project Harbor at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026 in Salt Lake City

The cloud-native community is heading back to Salt Lake City, Utah for **KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026**, running **November 9–12, 2026** at the Salt Palace Convention Center. ## Why Harbor Matters in 2026

The broader theme at KubeCon NA 2026 is production AI infrastructure — GPU scheduling, model deployment, agentic workloads, security, and cost management at scale. Harbor sits at the intersection of all of it: if you're distributing AI models and container images securely, managing artifacts at scale, or enforcing software supply chain policies in a regulated environment, Harbor is the registry layer you need.

Key areas Harbor addresses:

- **Artifact management** — images, Helm charts, OCI artifacts, AI models, SBOMs
- **Security scanning** — integrated Trivy, pluggable scanner adapters, vulnerability policy enforcement
- **Supply chain security** — cosign signing, SBOM attachment, artifact provenance
- **Multi-tenancy** — project-level RBAC, quotas, replication, and webhooks at scale
- **Registry federation** — proxy caching, cross-datacenter replication, geo-distribution

If any of that maps to a problem you're solving, find us in Salt Lake City.
